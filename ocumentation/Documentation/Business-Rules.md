# Business Rules

Business Rules were used to automate calculations and maintain consistent data in the application.

## Percentage Calculation

Calculates the student's percentage based on the academic marks.

## Grade Calculation

Assigns a grade based on the calculated percentage.

## Attendance Percentage

Calculates the student's attendance percentage based on attendance records.

## Fee Outstanding Calculation

Calculates the outstanding fee amount based on the student's fee details.
